Outbound IPs

What public IPs different probes see for your connection. Divergence here usually means split tunneling, a transparent proxy, or IPv4/IPv6 dual-stack quirks.

WebRTC UDP Exposure

STUN candidate gathering. Reveals local interface IPs (when mDNS is off) and your public UDP-reflexive address. If a VPN leaks here, real source IP shows up.

CDN Routing & POP

Which edge node each major CDN routes you to. Useful for diagnosing latency, anycast misroutes, and confirming which carrier hands you off to which provider.

DNS Resolver View

What public DNS-over-HTTPS resolvers report about your client. The browser already chose a resolver — these probes show what each would say if it answered for you.

IP Intelligence

Geolocation, ASN, ISP, organization, hosting-vs-residential signals, and proxy/VPN heuristics aggregated from multiple databases.

Browser & Network Hints

What your browser exposes locally: connection class, languages, timezone, platform, screen, and capabilities relevant to network probing.

Egresslyzer runs entirely in your browser. No data is stored; probes hit third-party public endpoints (Cloudflare, ipify, ipapi, ipwho, ifconfig.co, Google & Cloudflare DoH, ipinfo). Results are observational, not authoritative.
