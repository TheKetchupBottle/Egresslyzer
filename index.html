Egresslyzer

A browser-side check of how your network egresses to the public internet — local IPs, outbound IPv4/IPv6, DNS resolvers, CDN POP routing, WebRTC UDP exposure, and IP intelligence.

Source on GitHub →

Summary

Outbound IPs

Public IPs reported by independent endpoints. Divergence usually means split tunneling, a transparent proxy, or IPv4/IPv6 dual-stack quirks.

WebRTC UDP Exposure

STUN candidate gathering. Surfaces local interface IPs (when mDNS is off) and your UDP server-reflexive address. VPN leaks often show up here.

CDN Routing & POP

Which edge node each major CDN hands you to. Useful for diagnosing latency, anycast misroutes, and confirming carrier handoff.

DNS Resolver View

What public DoH resolvers report about your client. These are not your system resolver — they show what each would see if it answered for you.

IP Intelligence

Geolocation, ASN, ISP, organization, reverse DNS, and a hosting-vs-residential heuristic aggregated from multiple databases.

Browser & Network Hints

What your browser exposes locally: connection class, languages, timezone, platform, screen, and capabilities relevant to network probing.

Egresslyzer runs entirely client-side. No data is stored or proxied; probes hit only third-party public endpoints (Cloudflare, ipify, ipapi, ipwho, ifconfig.co, Google & Cloudflare DoH, ipinfo). Results are observational, not authoritative.
